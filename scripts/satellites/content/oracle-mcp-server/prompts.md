Finding your way around the database:

- Which schemas own tables, and how many tables does each one have?
- Which tables in the SALES schema hold customer data? Show me their columns first.
- Where do we store e-mail addresses and phone numbers?
- Which columns could contain personal data such as IBANs, tax IDs or birth dates?
- Explain how the ORDERS and ORDER_LINES tables are related.
- Which indexes exist on the ORDERS table?

Answering business questions:

- Who were our top 10 customers by revenue last quarter?
- Show the 50 most recent orders with customer name and total.
- How many orders did we receive per month this year?
- Which invoices are more than 30 days overdue, grouped by customer?
- Which products have not sold in the last six months?
- Which customers have not ordered in the last 12 months?
- What is the average order value per sales region this year?
- Compare this month's revenue with the same month last year.
